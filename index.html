Privacy Policy

majors23

Major System Memory Trainer  ·  Last updated March 2026

The short version majors23 collects no personal data, requires no account, and makes no network requests. Everything stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. majors23 does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any identifying details to use the app.

The only data the app stores is:

  • Your quiz statistics (accuracy and response times per number)
  • Saved digit sequences and visual story notes you create
  • Your display preferences (day / night mode)

All of this is stored locally on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults system. It never leaves your device.

2. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or transmit any data to third parties. There are no analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash-reporting services, or any other third-party code embedded in the app.

3. Network Access

majors23 makes no network requests of any kind. The app functions entirely offline. No data is sent to or received from any server.

4. Device Permissions

majors23 requests only the following system capability:

  • Speech synthesis — used for the Read Aloud feature in the Reference tab. Audio is generated on-device by iOS and is never recorded or transmitted.

No camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other permission is requested.

5. Children's Privacy

majors23 does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is suitable for all ages.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Using the "Clear Stats" button in the Stats tab to remove quiz history
  • Deleting individual saved entries in the Generate tab
  • Uninstalling the app, which removes all associated data from your device

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Since the app collects no data, any future changes are unlikely to affect your privacy in any way.
